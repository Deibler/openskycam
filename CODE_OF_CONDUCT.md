# Code of conduct

openskycam is built by people who like looking up. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented, is expected to make that a good experience for everyone else.

## Expected

- Be welcoming, patient and kind, especially to people new to cameras, Cloudflare or code.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Credit other people's work, and admit your own mistakes.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information, such as an address, camera location, footage or credentials, without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or continuing after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code privately, through GitHub:

- On an issue, pull request, discussion or comment, open its **⋯** menu, choose **Report content**, then **Report to repository admins**.
- For anything else, or if the problem involves the maintainer, use [GitHub's abuse report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. The person who reported is not identified to the person reported without consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what happens, in proportion to what happened: a private note, a public warning, hiding or editing content, a temporary ban from the project, or a permanent one. Content that breaks this code can be removed without notice.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
